AutoSci is not a shipped template, and the subsystem it demonstrated is gone.
llmwiki has no template registry, no configurable lifecycle profiles, no
workflow engine, no artifact store command, and no review approval command, so
this guide's walkthrough - `template init autosci`, `profile validate`,
`profile show`, `connector list`, `connector run`, `review list`, `review show`,
`review approve`, `workflow start`, `workflow status`, `workflow submit`, and
`artifact write` - taught commands and surfaces the product does not have.

A project has one profile, the built-in default: `concepts` under
`wiki/concepts` and `queries` under `wiki/queries`. There are no custom entity
types, no lifecycle preconditions, no pinned artifact references, and no
trust-gated workflow writes, so the research loop this page described cannot be
expressed in the current product. The walkthrough was removed rather than
rewritten: no library surface survives to name in its place.

What remains is the pipeline itself - ingest, compile, query, lint, and export -
available in-process through the SDK and over the wire through MCP:

- [SDK guide](/guides/sdk) for `createWiki()` and its methods.
- [MCP agent integration](/guides/mcp-agent-integration) for the tool list.
- [Open Knowledge Format](/guides/open-knowledge-format) for bundle export and import.
